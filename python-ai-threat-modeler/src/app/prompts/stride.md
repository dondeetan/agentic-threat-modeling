Use STRIDE categories consistently:
- Spoofing: identity impersonation or weak authentication.
- Tampering: unauthorized modification of data, requests, infrastructure, or configuration.
- Repudiation: missing auditability or non-repudiation for important actions.
- InformationDisclosure: unauthorized exposure of secrets, sensitive data, logs, or metadata.
- DenialOfService: resource exhaustion or availability loss.
- ElevationOfPrivilege: bypassing authorization boundaries or gaining broader access.
